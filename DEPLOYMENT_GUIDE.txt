BIOCORE SOLUTION WEBSITE — GODADDY CPANEL DEPLOYMENT
====================================================

PACKAGE CONTENTS
- index.html — Home / Who We Are
- products-specializations.html — products and category filtering
- contact.html — inquiry page
- send-inquiry.php — emails form submissions to both BioCore addresses
- assets/ — styles, scripts and images
- .htaccess — basic security, compression and caching settings
- sitemap.xml and robots.txt — search-engine discovery

UPLOAD
1. Back up the existing website before replacing it.
2. In GoDaddy cPanel, open File Manager and enter public_html.
3. Upload the CONTENTS of this folder into public_html. Keep .htaccess at the
   root. If hidden files are not visible, enable “Show Hidden Files” in File
   Manager settings.
4. Confirm that PHP is enabled for the hosting account (PHP 8.0 or newer is
   recommended).
5. Visit all three pages on desktop and mobile.

FORM ROUTING
- send-inquiry.php currently sends each inquiry to:
  mv@biocoresolution.us
  bt@biocoresolution.us
- The From address is website@biocoresolution.com. For the best deliverability,
  create that mailbox/forwarder in the hosting account or replace $fromEmail in
  send-inquiry.php with a valid mailbox on the hosted domain.
- Submit a real test from the live website. Confirm delivery to both inboxes and
  check spam folders. Some hosting plans restrict PHP mail(); if a test fails,
  ask GoDaddy to enable outgoing PHP mail or connect an authenticated SMTP
  service.
- This package emails submissions but does not store a database copy.

LAUNCH CHECKLIST
- Connect and verify the SSL certificate.
- Once HTTPS works, uncomment the HTTPS redirect lines at the bottom of
  .htaccess.
- Confirm the address and both email addresses.
- Have BioCore approve every public product category and sample example.
- Confirm that the current-site photography may be reused outside the former
  GoDaddy Website Builder before publishing. Replace any image that is not
  licensed for the new hosting setup.
- Do not add IRB, FDA, WHO, CLIA or other regulatory claims/logos without
  BioCore’s written approval and a legal right to use them.
- Add Google Analytics/Search Console only after IDs and a final privacy notice
  are approved. No analytics are installed in this package.
- Test current Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox, including keyboard navigation.
- Submit the form again after the domain and SSL changes are complete.

EDITING
- Page copy is inside the three .html files.
- Colors and layout are in assets/css/styles.css.
- Product filtering, mobile navigation and the cookie notice are in
  assets/js/main.js.
- Form recipients and the sender address are near the top of send-inquiry.php.

IMPORTANT
Availability is intentionally framed as subject to confirmation. This package
does not publish unapproved IRB, certification, worldwide-shipping, phone,
LinkedIn or guaranteed-turnaround claims.
